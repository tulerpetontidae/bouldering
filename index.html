What is my level?

The top of the chart is the mix of grades I attempt or send (switch on the right). Watch the roster shift as harder problems work their way in, a few at a time.

A level is hard to pin down in bouldering, so I track three. Top 5 sends, my favourite: the average of my five hardest sends. Consolidated: the hardest grade I've sent at least three times, so it's routine rather than luck. Hardest send: the single best one, for premature ego boosting. All three over a rolling 3-week window.
